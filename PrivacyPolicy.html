PropTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 March, 2026

Data Storage

PropTrack stores all your data locally on your device. No data is collected, transmitted, or shared with third parties.

Google Drive Backup (Optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, PropTrack creates a single data file in your Google Drive app folder. PropTrack does not access any other files in your Google Drive.

Your Google Client ID is stored locally on your device and is not shared.
